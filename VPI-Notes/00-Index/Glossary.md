# Glossary

## A
**ADR (Architecture Decision Record)**  
A short document capturing a significant architectural decision and its rationale.

## B
**Block**  
A building block in the VPI society layout containing multiple units.

## C
**Contribution**  
A monthly maintenance payment made by a unit owner or resident.

## I
**Individual**  
A person associated with a unit as an owner or resident.

## U
**Unit**  
A residential or commercial space within a block.
